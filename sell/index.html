How it works
From sign-up to first sale

The application takes about 10 minutes. Save at any step and finish later — your progress is kept.

Create your seller account

Sign up with your email or Google account at the Marcato Vendor Center.

Complete your application

Tell us about your store, verify your phone, add your ID details and payout account.

Get reviewed & approved

The Marcato team manually reviews every application. You'll see your status any time you sign in.

Start selling

Once approved, your store goes live and you can start listing products right away.

Every application is reviewed by a person. Submitting the form doesn't grant selling access by itself — an admin checks your details first and you'll be notified of the decision or asked for corrections.
